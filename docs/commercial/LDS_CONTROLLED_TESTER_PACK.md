# LUNDUS DIGITAL SYSTEMS — Controlled Tester Pack

Status: PRE-LAUNCH / NON-PRODUCTION
Target cohort: 5–10 invited testers

## Objective
Validate clarity, trust, workflow comprehension, mobile usability and quotation intent before public commercial activation.

## Tester rules
- No live payment.
- No Production customer charging.
- Do not submit real confidential client data.
- Use test/demo information only.
- Report any wording that suggests government database access, statutory approval or guaranteed outcome.
- Report any point where the workflow is unclear or requires explanation from the operator.

## Tester journey
1. Open Home.
2. Explain in one sentence what LUNDUS DIGITAL SYSTEMS appears to offer.
3. Open Services.
4. Identify the first commercial offer.
5. Open Contact.
6. Attempt to understand what information would be needed for a quotation.
7. Open FAQ.
8. Open Privacy, Terms, Refund and Maklumat Urus Niaga.
9. Open Checkout.
10. Confirm that live payment is visibly disabled.
11. On mobile viewport, repeat Home -> Services -> Contact -> Checkout.
12. Record friction, ambiguity, trust concerns and broken links.

## Acceptance questions
Each tester answers:
- What does this business sell?
- What is the primary action you would take?
- Did you see any claim that felt too strong or unclear?
- Could you understand whether payment is currently live?
- Could you find privacy/terms/refund/business information?
- Was mobile navigation usable?
- Would you know how to request a quotation?
- What information was missing before you would trust a paid transaction?

## Severity
- P0: misleading payment/legal/security claim, broken critical path, privacy/security risk.
- P1: blocks quotation intent or makes core offer unclear.
- P2: usability/copy/layout issue.
- P3: cosmetic preference.

## Exit criteria
Controlled tester phase may be marked PASS only when:
- no open P0 issues;
- no unresolved P1 issue on Home -> Services -> Contact -> legal/trust -> Checkout path;
- all required pages load;
- checkout remains visibly HOLD before licence/payment activation;
- feedback from at least 5 invited testers is recorded.

This pack does not authorize public launch or payment activation.
