# LD Controlled Marketing Validation Playbook v1

Status: PRE-LAUNCH / NON-PRODUCTION

Purpose: run the first LD marketing cohort as an evidence-gathering exercise, not as an uncontrolled scale campaign.

## Entry gates

The cohort must not be treated as ready for paid-order scale until:
- PR #296 Global Commerce Readiness is merged to main
- PR #317 Integrated Customer Lifecycle is merged to main
- PR #319 Global Demand Engine is merged to main
- applicable licence/payment readiness is confirmed
- supported-market evidence is current
- quotation/payment/delivery human gates remain intact

## Initial cohort target

100 relevant visitors
→ 10 qualified leads
→ 3–5 quotations
→ at least 1 paid customer
→ at least 1 evidence-backed case study

These are learning targets, not promised conversion rates.

## Evidence to capture

For each cohort:
- cohort ID and dates
- channel
- campaign / landing page
- relevant visitors
- leads captured
- qualified leads
- quotations issued
- paid customers
- accepted deliveries
- referrals
- repeat / expansion customers
- evidence-backed case studies
- paid media spend where applicable
- median first-response time
- median lead-to-quotation time
- blocker / loss reason categories

## Decision loop

1. Calculate conversion at each stage.
2. Identify the largest material funnel leak.
3. Check evidence before changing messaging, offer, channel or process.
4. Change only the smallest relevant part.
5. Run the next cohort.
6. Scale spend only after downstream delivery and proof are stable.

## Stop conditions

Pause or fail closed when:
- unsupported market is detected
- consent / privacy requirements are unresolved
- fraud or payment abuse signals are unresolved
- quotation or payment authority is unavailable
- delivery capacity is insufficient
- customer acceptance or proof evidence is unreliable
- CAC / spend is being measured without attributable qualified leads

## Scale rule

Do not scale because traffic is high.

Scale only when:
- qualified-lead quality is acceptable
- quotation and paid conversion are attributable
- delivery acceptance is stable
- case-study proof is verifiable
- referral / repeat signals begin to appear
- downstream commercial gates are operational
