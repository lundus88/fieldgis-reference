# LD Support & Maintenance Plans v1

Status: DEVELOPMENT / NON-PRODUCTION

Purpose: convert suitable completed projects into governed recurring support revenue without inventing service levels or hiding third-party costs.

## Planning tiers

### ESSENTIAL CARE
Baseline maintenance, monitored support intake and agreed maintenance scope.

### BUSINESS CARE
Broader support coverage plus planned small improvements where explicitly included.

### MANAGED SYSTEM
Higher-touch operational support, monitoring and governed improvement allocation.

These are commercial frameworks, not published prices. Final price and service commitments require human approval.

## Important boundaries

- response target is different from resolution target;
- no SLA claim is published without real operational evidence;
- third-party hosting/API/model/storage/domain costs remain explicit;
- recurring charges require customer acceptance;
- defects, regressions and agreed-scope remediation are not automatically paid enhancements;
- enhancements outside the plan route through Change Request or a new quotation.

No Production activation or customer billing is authorized here.
