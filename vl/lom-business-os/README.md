# LOM 3.0 — AI Business Operating System

Status: DEVELOPMENT / NON-PRODUCTION

Purpose: extend LOM 2.0 with evidence-bound portfolio intelligence for KPI/ROI observation, prioritisation advice, resource-allocation recommendations, opportunity/delivery learning, and institutional memory.

Operating rules:
- business recommendations are advisory only;
- no autonomous spending, pricing, contracting or customer commitment;
- no synthetic revenue, ROI, eligibility or demand evidence;
- missing/contradictory business evidence => HOLD/REVIEW;
- recommendations must cite supplied evidence dimensions;
- policy changes are PROPOSE_ONLY;
- consequential actions remain HUMAN_ONLY.
