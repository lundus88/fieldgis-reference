# LOM P6 Revenue & Execution Loop

Status: DEVELOPMENT / NON-PRODUCTION

Purpose: convert observed opportunities and portfolio work into evidence-bound economic recommendations without granting autonomous financial, contractual, production, or protected-main authority.

Core loop:
Opportunity -> Qualify -> Score -> Recommend -> Human decision when required -> Execute within delegated scope -> Verify outcome -> Learn

P6 considers revenue potential, probability of success, strategic value, urgency, delivery risk, effort/capacity cost, evidence freshness, and evidence quality.

Every active opportunity or project recommendation should expose one next-best-action.

Governance:
- missing or stale evidence fails closed to REVIEW or HOLD;
- scores do not authorize spending, pricing commitments, contracts, production actions, or protected-main merges;
- financial and contractual commitments remain HUMAN_ONLY;
- P6 may prepare decision packages but may not fabricate opportunity value, probability, customer intent, eligibility, or revenue.

Primary measures: revenue generated, conversion rate, human hours saved, safe autonomous completion rate, and Director intervention rate.
