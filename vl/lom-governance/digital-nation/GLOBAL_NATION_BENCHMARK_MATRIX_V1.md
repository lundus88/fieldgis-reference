## Purpose

Compare 20–30 national systems at the **capability level**, across 30+ fundamentals, without producing an overall country ranking or political-system winner.

## Evidence source families

The matrix should anchor research to current official/multilateral datasets where relevant, including:

- World Bank Worldwide Governance Indicators (governance)
- OECD Digital Government Index (digital public administration)
- UN E-Government Survey / EGDI (digital services and infrastructure)
- WIPO Global Innovation Index (innovation systems)
- ITU ICT Development Index (connectivity)
- World Bank Logistics Performance Index where applicable (logistics)
- WHO UHC indicators (health-system access)
- national statistical offices and primary legislation/policy documents
- sector-specific regulators and official ministries/agencies

No single index should determine an absorption decision.

## Decision method

A capability can move from research to absorption only when:
- evidence is sufficient and current enough for the question;
- scope/population/time period are explicit;
- material trade-offs are recorded;
- duplication is cleared;
- LOM owner is identified;
- security/legal/economic/governance reviews are complete;
- integration is reversible or has a defined exit strategy;
- post-integration measures are defined.
