# LOM Virtual World — Society Essentials v1

Status: DRAFT / NON-PRODUCTION  
Date: 2026-09-29  
Owner: LOM Core Governance

## Objective

Complete the Virtual World beyond commerce so members can reliably **find, communicate, belong, participate, protect their data, access services, move between digital spaces, recover from incidents and build on the platform**.

These are platform capabilities only. They do not create sovereign, statutory or public-authority powers.

## Essential capabilities

### 1. World Search & Navigation
One universal discovery layer across:
- people and public profiles;
- businesses;
- services;
- jobs and opportunities;
- learning;
- digital products;
- virtual spaces;
- events;
- help and policies.

Rules:
- permissions and privacy are respected;
- private objects never become searchable by default;
- ranking must be explainable where materially consequential;
- sponsored placement must be labelled.

High value:
- makes a large world usable;
- reduces time-to-outcome;
- connects otherwise isolated LOM capabilities.

### 2. World Directory & Places
Canonical directory for:
- Market;
- Academy;
- Business District;
- Opportunity Exchange;
- Community spaces;
- Creator spaces;
- support/service desks;
- member/business virtual spaces.

A place is a governed digital destination, not a real land parcel or statutory address.

### 3. Community & Groups
Members may create or join bounded communities such as:
- professional groups;
- learning cohorts;
- creator groups;
- business networks;
- project teams;
- interest communities.

Controls:
- role-based moderation;
- reporting;
- privacy/visibility levels;
- anti-spam/abuse controls;
- no automatic governance authority.

### 4. Events & Calendar
Unified event layer for:
- classes;
- workshops;
- product launches;
- business sessions;
- community events;
- project milestones;
- deadlines;
- appointment links.

Reuse existing notification/calendar adapters where available.

### 5. Member Service Center
A single place to:
- ask for help;
- check transaction/order status;
- raise a dispute;
- manage account/security issues;
- find procedures;
- route to UrusMY or the correct domain system;
- track support cases.

It is a platform service center, not a government counter.

### 6. Privacy & Data Control Center
Every member should be able to understand and control:
- profile visibility;
- consent;
- connected services;
- marketing preferences;
- evidence sharing;
- session/device access;
- exportable personal data where supported;
- account closure workflow subject to lawful/audit retention.

Principle:
**minimum necessary data, purpose limitation, explainability, revocable consent where applicable.**

### 7. Household, Team & Organization Relationships
Support structured relationships such as:
- household/family group;
- parent/guardian relationships where applicable;
- business teams;
- project teams;
- school/class cohorts;
- organization memberships.

No relationship may silently grant financial, identity or privacy authority.

### 8. Member Voice & Transparency Center
Founder-led governance remains unchanged.

Members may:
- submit suggestions;
- provide feedback;
- raise petitions/issues;
- comment on proposed product changes where invited;
- view policy change logs;
- view explanations of material platform decisions;
- use appeal channels.

These mechanisms are consultative and do not constitute binding elections or sovereign political rights.

### 9. Culture, Media & Creator Hub
For:
- member-created media;
- educational content;
- showcases;
- digital exhibitions;
- performances/events;
- community stories;
- approved livestream or recorded content;
- creator monetization through LD Commerce.

Rights/provenance and content policy apply.

### 10. Accessibility & Language Layer
Platform-wide support should include:
- BM and English baseline;
- additional languages/localization as demand proves;
- readable typography;
- keyboard/accessibility semantics;
- captions/transcripts where relevant;
- reduced-motion support;
- mobile-first low-bandwidth flows.

Accessibility is a platform requirement, not a separate product.

### 11. Safety, Incident & Resilience Center
Members need one place for:
- account compromise;
- fraud/payment concern;
- abusive content;
- service outage;
- lost access;
- security alerts;
- recovery status.

Operationally compose:
LOM Trust + Security + SRE/observability + recovery controls.

No unverified incident should be presented as confirmed.

### 12. Developer & Partner Platform
A controlled ecosystem for:
- documented APIs;
- adapters;
- partner integrations;
- approved extensions;
- webhooks;
- sandbox/test credentials;
- versioned contracts;
- capability discovery.

Rules:
- least privilege;
- scoped permissions;
- explicit consent;
- audit trail;
- no partner may bypass LOM authority/policy boundaries.

High value:
- expands capability without LOM building everything itself;
- creates ecosystem/network effects.

### 13. Data Portability & Interoperability
Where technically and legally appropriate:
- export member-owned data;
- import approved formats;
- portable evidence references;
- stable identifiers;
- versioned APIs/contracts;
- standard interchange formats.

No export should leak another member's private data or protected platform secrets.

### 14. World Announcements & Verified Information
A provenance-backed channel for:
- platform notices;
- maintenance;
- policy changes;
- new features;
- safety notices;
- institution announcements.

Official platform announcements must be distinguishable from member content.

### 15. Personal Home / Dashboard
Every member receives a private home view summarizing:
- tasks;
- learning;
- opportunities;
- orders;
- earnings evidence;
- businesses;
- messages;
- events;
- alerts;
- next recommended actions.

This is a composition surface, not a new engine.

- World Directory & Places

- Safety, Incident & Resilience Center
- Personal Home / Dashboard

### P1 — world becomes social and participatory
- Community & Groups
- Events & Calendar
- Household, Team & Organization Relationships
- Member Voice & Transparency Center
- Culture, Media & Creator Hub

### P2 — ecosystem and portability
- Developer & Partner Platform
- Data Portability & Interoperability
- advanced World Announcements / information syndication

## Success metrics

Measure:
- successful search-to-action rate;
- time to find destination/service;
- support resolution time;
- privacy-control completion;
- account recovery success;
- accessibility defects;
- active groups/events;
- member return frequency;
- cross-capability journeys;
- partner integrations;
- data export/import success;
- incident recovery time.

## Principle

**A virtual world becomes credible when people can orient themselves, control their data, communicate, form communities, access help, participate safely and move between useful destinations without friction.**
