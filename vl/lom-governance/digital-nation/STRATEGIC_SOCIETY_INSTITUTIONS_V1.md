# LOM Virtual World — Strategic Society Institutions v1

Status: DRAFT / NON-PRODUCTION  
Date: 2026-09-29  
Owner: LOM Core Governance

## Objective

Add the institutional capacities a durable society needs beyond market, education, work and community.

These are **private-platform institutions and control layers**. They do not create sovereign powers, statutory ministries, diplomatic recognition, police powers, public taxation, courts of law, passports, legal tender, or regulatory licences.

The design goal is institutional strength:

**continuity -> infrastructure -> integrity -> standards -> resilience -> knowledge -> innovation -> trusted partnerships**

## Strategic institutions

### 1. Charter, Policy & Rights Registry
Canonical home for:
- LOM Charter;
- member rights;
- platform rules;
- authority boundaries;
- policy versions;
- effective dates;
- change history;
- appeal rights.

Every consequential rule should be versioned and attributable.

High value:
- reduces arbitrary behavior;
- improves trust;
- gives automation a stable policy source.

### 2. Continuity & Succession Protocol
Defines what happens when the Founder or a critical human authority cannot act.

Must cover:
- temporary delegation;
- emergency-only delegated authority;
- expiry/revocation;
- quorum or secondary approval where needed;
- recovery of authority;
- complete audit trail.

No AI may appoint itself as successor or expand its own authority.

### 3. Integrity & Inspector Function
Independent review capability for:
- conflicts of interest;
- procurement integrity;
- misuse of authority;
- financial irregularities;
- privileged-access abuse;
- policy exceptions;
- retaliation concerns.

It should report evidence and findings, not invent accusations.

### 4. Critical Digital Infrastructure & Utilities
Treat these as essential services:
- identity/authentication;
- authorization;
- storage;
- compute/runtime;
- notifications/communications;
- payments connectivity;
- evidence/audit store;
- backup/recovery;
- search/navigation.

Required properties:
- health monitoring;
- redundancy where practical;
- incident ownership;
- recovery targets;
- dependency mapping;
- no silent fallback to excluded infrastructure.

### 5. Emergency Management & Continuity Center
For platform-wide events such as:
- major outage;
- compromised credentials;
- payment-provider failure;
- data-integrity incident;
- security event;
- large-scale fraud campaign;
- critical dependency failure.

Flow:
Detect -> Classify -> Contain -> Communicate -> Recover -> Verify -> Post-incident review.

No unverified incident should be declared as confirmed.

### 6. Records, Archives & Institutional Memory
Canonical archive for:
- policy records;
- institutional decisions;
- releases;
- contracts;
- evidence;
- major incidents;
- governance changes;
- historical metrics;
- public-facing institutional history.

Records must preserve provenance and retention policy.

### 7. Standards, Quality & Accreditation
Platform standards for:
- service quality;
- learning/skill verification;
- digital product quality;
- seller/business requirements;
- security;
- accessibility;
- data interchange;
- evidence integrity.

Any badge/certificate is a **platform credential**, not a statutory professional licence unless backed by a real authorized external issuer.

### 8. Public-Interest Knowledge & Open Information Hub
Curated access to:
- official LOM policies;
- guides;
- institutional data suitable for publication;
- economic indicators;
- learning resources;
- platform service directories;
- safety information;
- developer documentation.

All published statistics must identify scope, date and methodology.

### 9. Research, Science & Innovation System
A governed pipeline for:
- research;
- experiments;
- new product concepts;
- AI capability evaluation;
- geospatial/scientific work;
- education innovation;
- commercialization candidates.

Flow:
Problem -> Research -> Evidence -> Prototype -> QA -> Controlled Pilot -> Product/Policy Decision.

Research results are not automatically Production authority.

### 10. Strategic Projects & Public-Works Analogue
Registry for high-impact platform projects such as:
- new economic infrastructure;
- major education initiatives;
- large data migrations;
- platform-wide security upgrades;
- major virtual-world spaces;
- shared member services.

Each strategic project should have:
- owner;
- objective;
- budget;
- milestones;
- dependencies;
- risk;
- evidence;
- acceptance criteria;
- post-completion review.

This is platform project governance, not state public works.

### 11. Procurement & Vendor System
For acquiring:
- cloud services;
- software;
- data services;
- professional services;
- licensed content;
- infrastructure;
- operational tools.

Controls:
- need/requirement;
- quotation/comparison evidence;
- conflict disclosure;
- budget authority;
- contract record;
- delivery verification;
- renewal/termination tracking.

No autonomous financial commitment without approved authority.

### 12. Supply, Logistics & Fulfilment Coordination
For products/services requiring external delivery or third parties:
- fulfilment status;
- provider routing;
- digital delivery;
- future physical logistics adapters;
- SLA evidence;
- exception handling.

It must not impersonate a postal, customs or transport authority.

### 13. Strategic Reserves & Recovery Resources
Platform resilience may maintain:
- operating reserves;
- emergency service credits;
- redundant backups;
- standby infrastructure;
- emergency vendor options;
- recovery runbooks.

Financial reserves remain company/platform assets, not sovereign reserves.

### 14. Sustainability & Resource Efficiency
Track where useful:
- compute consumption;
- storage growth;
- infrastructure efficiency;
- vendor/resource concentration risk;
- wasteful duplicate capability;
- lifecycle/deprecation.

Do not fabricate carbon/environmental claims without evidence.

### 15. External Partnerships & Interoperability
Formal partner layer for:
- technology providers;
- payment/accounting providers;
- education partners;
- professional networks;
- commerce partners;
- research institutions;
- logistics providers;
- regulated service providers.

This is commercial/platform partnership, not diplomatic recognition or treaty-making.

### 16. Member Rights & Ombuds Channel
Independent escalation path for:
- unresolved support issues;
- disputed platform decisions;
- access restrictions;
- privacy concerns;
- unfair-process concerns;
- appeal-status tracking.

The Ombuds function can recommend correction/review within platform authority. It is not a court.

### 17. Youth, Family & Vulnerable-User Safety
Where minors or vulnerable users are supported, the platform must apply:
- age-appropriate design;
- guardian/parent relationships where legally appropriate;
- restricted commerce/communications;
- privacy defaults;
- content safeguards;
- escalation to real-world services where required.

No relationship may be inferred without evidence.

### 18. National-Scale Analytics Analogue
A privacy-preserving institutional dashboard for:
- active population;
- skills;
- education outcomes;
- opportunities;
- business activity;
- transaction activity;
- member earnings;
- service reliability;
- disputes;
- safety incidents;
- platform revenue/cost.

This is a platform society dashboard, not an official government census.

## Core constitutional boundary

LOM Virtual World may have institutions that **function like the administrative organs of a country inside the platform**, but it must remain clear that:

- membership is not legal citizenship;
- Founder authority is platform authority;
- LOM rules are platform rules;
- LOM dispute outcomes are platform decisions;
- LOM credentials are platform credentials unless externally authorized;
- LOM Treasury is company/platform finance;
- LOM Market is private commerce;
- external relationships are partnerships, not diplomacy;
- enforcement is limited to lawful platform controls.

## Principle

**A strong virtual society needs more than commerce. It needs continuity, records, integrity, infrastructure, emergency response, standards, research, accountable authority and institutional memory.**
