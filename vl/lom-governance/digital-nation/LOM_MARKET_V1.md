# LOM Virtual World — LOM Market v1

Status: DRAFT / NON-PRODUCTION  
Date: 2026-09-29  
Canonical owner: LD Commerce + LOM Trust + LOM Core policy  
Surface name: **LOM Market**

## Purpose

LOM Market is the economic marketplace of LOM Virtual World.

It is a unified market surface where members and platform businesses may lawfully discover, offer, buy, sell, commission and deliver value using existing LOM/LD capabilities.

LOM Market is **not** a new payment processor, CRM, accounting engine, bank, employment authority or statutory market regulator.

## Market structure

### 1. Service Market
For professional and digital services:
- website/software work;
- design/content;
- remote support;
- consulting;
- education/tutoring;
- surveying/geospatial digital services where lawful;
- other approved service categories.

### 2. Work & Gig Market
Composes the Opportunity Exchange and Economic Participation Network:
- microtasks;
- gigs;
- project work;
- freelance assignments;
- creator work;
- specialist work.

### 3. Digital Goods & IP Market
For:
- templates;
- software;
- learning products;
- designs;
- documents;
- datasets where rights permit;
- licensed digital assets.

Every item requires provenance, licence/usage terms and delivery evidence.

### 4. Business-to-Business Market
For business demand and supply:
- service requests;
- quotations;
- procurement opportunities;
- subcontracting;
- partnership opportunities.

### 5. Virtual Asset Market
For platform-native entitlements such as:
- virtual storefronts;
- virtual offices;
- classrooms;
- project spaces;
- licensed world assets.

These are digital platform rights only and never statutory land/property title.

### 6. Future Physical-Goods Bridge
Physical goods may be added later through approved commerce, logistics, tax and consumer-protection adapters.

P0 does not require a physical-goods marketplace.

## Canonical transaction

Discover
→ Listing/Request
→ Qualification
→ Offer/Quotation
→ Order
→ Payment confirmation
→ Delivery
→ Acceptance
→ Earnings/Platform fee
→ Reputation evidence
→ Repeat/Referral.

This reuses the existing Golden Transaction / Golden World Transaction model.

## Listing contract

Every listing must identify:
- seller/business identity;
- listing type;
- title and description;
- category;
- price mode;
- currency where applicable;
- scope/deliverables;
- fulfilment method;
- expected delivery period;
- cancellation/refund terms;
- rights/licence terms where applicable;
- evidence/provenance references where material;
- policy classification.

Unsupported or materially ambiguous listings remain HOLD.

## Price modes

P0:
- FIXED_PRICE
- REQUEST_QUOTE
- PACKAGE_PRICE

P1:
- NEGOTIATED
- SUBSCRIPTION
- MILESTONE

P2, only after separate review:
- bounded auction/tender mechanisms where lawful and suitable.

No deceptive scarcity, fabricated discounts, hidden mandatory fees or invented reference prices.

## Market trust controls

LOM Market must:
- verify seller/member authority before consequential actions;
- separate identity from public reputation;
- preserve listing/version history;
- retain order/payment/delivery evidence;
- reject fabricated testimonials/results;
- route disputes to LOM Trust;
- use evidence-backed reputation;
- support cancellation/refund rules;
- prevent duplicate fulfilment and duplicate payout;
- apply fraud/payment-abuse controls;
- fail closed on unresolved authority.

## Market safety boundary

Listings must be rejected or held where prohibited by platform policy or law.

LOM Market must not become a venue for:
- unlawful goods/services;
- unlicensed regulated financial products;
- false government/statutory services;
- counterfeit or stolen goods;
- harmful or prohibited commerce;
- fabricated credentials or ownership;
- deceptive financial/investment claims.

## Market discovery

Discovery may use:
- category;
- member needs;
- verified skills;
- location/market eligibility where relevant;
- language;
- price range;
- delivery time;
- availability;
- evidence-backed trust signals.

Ranking must not be pay-to-win unless sponsored placement is explicitly labelled.

Material recommendations should remain explainable.

## Market economics

Track:
- active buyers;
- active sellers;
- active listings;
- qualified demand;
- orders;
- gross transaction value;
- platform revenue;
- seller earnings;
- contribution margin;
- repeat purchase rate;
- time to first sale;
- fulfilment time;
- acceptance rate;
- refund/dispute rate;
- fraud/abuse loss;
- buyer/seller retention.

## P0 Market Square

The first visible market should contain four entry points:

1. **Hire a Service**
2. **Find Work**
3. **Buy Digital Products**
4. **Start / Grow a Business**

A single search bar may search across the four surfaces while preserving category-specific eligibility and policy rules.

## P0 acceptance

P0 is ready for controlled Preview only when:
- seller identity/authority can be checked;
- a listing can be created and versioned;
- buyer can discover and qualify an offer;
- order is created from authoritative price/scope;
- payment remains provider-evidence based;
- delivery and acceptance are auditable;
- earnings/fees are traceable;
- reputation evidence is produced;
- dispute/refund path is testable;
- cross-user isolation passes;
- no Production activation occurs automatically.

## Principle

**LOM Market should make it easy for a member to convert a need, skill, product or business capability into a trusted transaction.**
