# LOM Virtual World — Operating Model v1

Status: DRAFT / NON-PRODUCTION  
Date: 2026-09-29  
Owner: LOM Core Governance

## 1. Vision

LOM Virtual World is a persistent digital society where members can perform many of the same categories of activity that exist in the physical world:

- establish a digital identity;
- learn and gain credentials;
- work and earn;
- create and operate businesses;
- buy and sell products and services;
- hold and transfer governed digital assets;
- enter contracts;
- build reputation;
- resolve disputes;
- participate in community governance;
- access digital public-like services;
- contribute to and benefit from a transparent economic ecosystem.

LOM Virtual World is not a sovereign country, government, central bank, court of law, land authority, immigration authority or tax authority. All real-world legal rights, obligations, ownership and regulated activity remain subject to applicable jurisdictions.

## 5. Virtual institutions

LOM Virtual World may expose institutions as product surfaces, while keeping their legal nature clear:

1. **LOM Registry Office** — identity and organization records.
2. **LOM Commerce House** — marketplace and transactions.
3. **LOM Work Exchange** — jobs, gigs and economic participation.
4. **LOM Academy** — learning and skills.
5. **LOM Civic Desk** — UrusMY-led assistance.
6. **LOM Asset Registry** — digital entitlements and virtual spaces.
7. **LOM Trust Tribunal** — platform disputes and appeals.
8. **LOM Treasury Office** — platform budgets, fees and economic records.
9. **LOM Statistics Office** — transparent ecosystem metrics.
10. **LOM Security & Trust Center** — abuse, fraud and incident handling.

These are internal platform institutions, not public authorities.

## 6. Currency and value model

P0 and P1:
- use real-world regulated payment rails;
- record value through auditable transaction ledgers;
- allow internal non-cash points/credits only when clearly denominated as platform benefits;
- prohibit representing internal credits as legal tender.

A crypto/token economy is not part of P0. It requires a separate legal, financial, security and regulatory programme.

## 7. Governance model

LOM Core remains the constitutional control plane.

Authority hierarchy:
1. applicable law;
2. platform charter;
3. security / trust policy;
4. human authority gates;
5. service/domain policy;
6. automated execution within granted authority.

AI may recommend, classify, route and execute bounded actions. AI may not grant itself authority, redefine the charter, create money, impose real taxes, issue statutory licences or create real-world legal title.

## 9. Golden World Transaction

A complete test must show:

**New member → creates profile → gains skill signal → sees opportunity → joins/creates business → receives customer order → completes governed payment → delivers → customer accepts → member earns → platform records commission/revenue → reputation updates → member reinvests or takes next opportunity.**

All transitions must be auditable.

## 10. World-scale roadmap

### Phase 1 — Founding
Charter, identity, business registry, opportunity board, marketplace, trust model.

### Phase 2 — Economy
Payments, affiliates, jobs, business operations, recurring income, economic dashboard.

### Phase 3 — Society
Education, community governance, dispute resolution, support programmes, virtual asset spaces.

### Phase 4 — Persistent World
Member-to-member economy, organizations, long-lived digital spaces, APIs and interoperable identity/capability records.

### Phase 5 — Global Network
Multi-language, multi-currency, cross-border commerce, partner ecosystems, regulated adapters and global digital participation.
