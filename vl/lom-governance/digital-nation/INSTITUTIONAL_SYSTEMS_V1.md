# LOM Virtual World — Institutional Systems v1

Status: DRAFT / NON-PRODUCTION  
Date: 2026-09-29  
Owner: LOM Core Governance

## 1. Objective

Define the minimum institutional systems required for LOM Virtual World to function as a persistent digital society and economy.

These systems are platform institutions only. They do not represent sovereign government authority, statutory licensing, legal tender, regulated banking, courts of law, or public registries.

## 2. Institutional system map

### A. LOM Financial System
Purpose:
- receive and route lawful payments;
- maintain internal transaction ledgers;
- issue receipts and accounting evidence;
- track balances owed, commissions and earnings;
- support payouts through regulated payment providers;
- manage platform budgets and reserves.

P0 rule:
- no deposit-taking;
- no unlicensed lending;
- no investment products;
- no claim to be a bank;
- no legal-tender currency;
- use regulated external payment rails and banking partners.

Future regulated capabilities may be added only through licensed partners and legal/compliance review.

### B. LOM Economic System
Purpose:
- marketplace;
- digital products;
- professional services;
- subscriptions;
- affiliate/referral economy;
- creator economy;
- B2B commerce;
- business creation and business profiles;
- pricing, commissions and platform fees;
- economic analytics.

Primary loop:

Opportunity → Offer → Transaction → Delivery → Acceptance → Earnings → Reputation → Repeat → Reinvestment.

### C. LOM Education System
Owner: LOM Education.

Purpose:
- diagnostic learning;
- personalized learning paths;
- skill graph;
- assessments;
- mastery tracking;
- platform credentials;
- learning-to-work matching;
- lifelong learning.

Primary loop:

Learn → Practice → Assess → Verify Skill → Match Opportunity → Work → Earn → Upskill.

### D. LOM Employment & Opportunity System
Purpose:
- jobs;
- gigs;
- projects;
- tenders/opportunities where lawful;
- creator work;
- affiliate opportunities;
- specialist matching;
- business-to-business opportunities.

LundusLead and the Economic Participation Network should supply discovery and qualification. This system must not duplicate an existing CRM.

### E. LOM Business System
Purpose:
- platform Business Registry;
- business profiles;
- team roles;
- service catalogues;
- customer intake;
- orders;
- quotation;
- payment;
- delivery;
- accounting;
- customer acceptance;
- support and repeat sales.

Platform registration does not replace SSM, local-authority licensing, tax registration or any statutory requirement.

### F. LOM Identity & Civil Society System
Purpose:
- LOM Member ID;
- identity assurance;
- profile;
- consent;
- privacy controls;
- household/team/org relationships;
- membership status;
- reputation signals;
- lifecycle records.

Member identity must be separated from public-facing reputation data.

### G. LOM Treasury System
Purpose:
- platform revenue;
- commissions;
- approved grants/incentives;
- budgets;
- reserves;
- operating expenditure;
- project profitability;
- audit trail.

Treasury activity remains company/platform finance, not sovereign public finance.

### H. LOM Justice & Dispute System
Purpose:
- complaints;
- evidence intake;
- mediation;
- platform decisions;
- appeals;
- fraud and abuse cases;
- commercial disputes inside the platform.

This is private platform dispute resolution, not a court of law.

### I. LOM Trust, Safety & Security System
Purpose:
- identity abuse;
- fraud prevention;
- payment abuse;
- marketplace abuse;
- account protection;
- access control;
- incident response;
- recovery;
- audit;
- sanctions within platform authority.

### J. LOM Digital Asset & Virtual Property System
Purpose:
- digital storefronts;
- virtual offices;
- project spaces;
- licensed digital assets;
- provenance;
- entitlement;
- transfer history.

No virtual title may be represented as statutory ownership of real land or physical property.

### K. LOM Civic Assistance System
Owner: UrusMY and domain adapters.

Purpose:
- explain real-world procedures;
- prepare checklists and documents;
- route users to proper authorities;
- track deadlines and evidence;
- assist with civic and administrative workflows.

No claim of government-database access unless a real authorized integration exists.

### L. LOM Health & Wellbeing Access Layer
Purpose:
- education;
- wellbeing resources;
- appointment/service routing through permitted partners;
- emergency guidance to real-world services where appropriate.

It must not impersonate a health authority or replace licensed clinicians.

### M. LOM Communications System
Purpose:
- member inbox;
- notifications;
- email/messaging adapters;
- business communication;
- support;
- community channels;
- consent-controlled marketing.

### N. LOM Statistics & Intelligence System
Purpose:
- population/activity metrics;
- business activity;
- marketplace activity;
- employment/opportunity metrics;
- education outcomes;
- member earnings;
- revenue;
- retention;
- disputes;
- fraud loss;
- delivery performance.

Analytics must preserve privacy and avoid deceptive population/economic claims.

## 6. Architectural rule

Do not create standalone duplicate engines. New institutional surfaces should compose existing LOM capabilities:

- LOM Core = governance, authority, audit, security, orchestration;
- LundusLead = acquisition, opportunity discovery, growth;
- LD = commerce, Product Factory, delivery and business operations;
- LOM Education = learning and skill intelligence;
- UrusMY = civic-assistance workflows;
- domain systems = specialist services;
- adapters = payment, banking, accounting, messaging and external regulated services.

## 7. Golden Society Transaction

The first complete proof:

New member
→ creates verified profile
→ learns or proves a skill
→ receives a qualified opportunity
→ joins or creates a platform business
→ receives an order
→ customer pays through regulated rail
→ work is delivered
→ customer accepts
→ earnings and platform revenue are recorded
→ reputation evidence updates
→ member receives the next opportunity.

## 8. Core principle

**A functioning virtual society is not defined by buildings or titles. It is defined by trusted identity, useful institutions, real economic activity, enforceable platform rules, education, opportunity and repeat participation.**


### O. LOM Market
Purpose:
- provide the main economic marketplace of the Virtual World;
- connect buyers, sellers, workers, creators and businesses;
- compose Service Market, Work/Gig Market, Digital Goods/IP Market, B2B Market and Virtual Asset Market;
- route transactions through the existing Golden Transaction / payment / delivery / acceptance / trust stack.

P0 market entry points:
1. Hire a Service
2. Find Work
3. Buy Digital Products
4. Start / Grow a Business

LOM Market is a commerce surface and orchestration layer, not a second payment, CRM, accounting, reputation or dispute engine.


### P. LOM World Search & Navigation
Purpose:
- provide universal search across public/authorized people, businesses, services, work, learning, products, events and virtual places;
- preserve permissions and privacy;
- route users directly to useful actions.

### Q. LOM Privacy & Data Control Center
Purpose:
- consent and profile visibility;
- connected-service permissions;
- evidence-sharing controls;
- session/device review;
- supported data export/account closure workflows;
- privacy explanations and audit references.

Private data is not searchable or public by default.

### R. LOM Community & Groups
Purpose:
- professional, learning, creator, business, project and interest communities;
- role-based moderation;
- member reporting and trust controls;
- public/private visibility.

Community roles do not automatically grant platform governance, financial or identity authority.

### S. LOM Events & Calendar
Purpose:
- classes, workshops, launches, community events, appointments, milestones and deadlines;
- event discovery;
- notifications and reminders through existing adapters.

### T. LOM Member Service Center
Purpose:
- support;
- account/security recovery routing;
- order/transaction status;
- dispute entry;
- procedure help;
- routing to UrusMY and domain systems.

### U. LOM Member Voice & Transparency Center
Purpose:
- suggestions, feedback and non-binding proposals;
- policy/change logs;
- explanations of material platform decisions;
- appeal routing.

Founder-led governance remains unchanged; this is a consultative transparency surface, not an electoral legislature.

### V. LOM Culture, Media & Creator Hub
Purpose:
- creator showcases;
- educational/media content;
- digital exhibitions and events;
- lawful monetization through LD Commerce;
- provenance and rights evidence.

### W. LOM Developer & Partner Platform
Purpose:
- APIs;
- adapters;
- webhooks;
- partner integrations;
- sandbox contracts;
- scoped permissions and auditability.

No partner integration may bypass LOM policy or authority boundaries.

### X. LOM Safety, Incident & Resilience Center
Purpose:
- account compromise;
- fraud/payment concerns;
- abuse reports;
- service outages;
- recovery status;
- verified security notices.

Composes LOM Trust, Security, Observability and Recovery capabilities.

## 10. Twelve-fundamental institutional alignment

LOM Virtual World now uses the following advanced-society alignment:

| Fundamental | LOM institutional owner/composition |
|---|---|
| Rule of Law & Institutions | Charter, Policy & Rights Registry, LOM Core Governance, Trust Tribunal, Ombuds, Audit |
| Productive Economy | LOM Market, LD Commerce, Business Engine, Opportunity Exchange, Economic Participation |
| Financial & Capital System | Regulated payment/accounting/capital adapters + Treasury controls |
| Education & Human Capital | LOM Education + Skill-to-Income Graph |
| Science, Technology & Innovation | LOM R&D, Knowledge Foundation, HVAE, Product Factory |
| Infrastructure | LOM Platform/SRE, VPS, storage, communications, search, recovery |
| Administrative Capacity | Registries, workflows, case tracking, statistics, policy orchestration |
| Security & Resilience | LOM Trust, Security, SRE, incident/recovery |
| Health & Social Protection | Wellbeing access + retraining/opportunity/approved support pathways |
| Financial Management | LOM Treasury, Accounting Adapter, budgets, procurement, project profitability |
| Global Economic Relations | Global Commerce Bridge, localization, partner platform and lawful cross-border adapters |
| Continuous Improvement | System Health, CAIE, Evidence Lineage, Completion Governance, HVAE |

Important boundaries:
- financial/capital functions that require licences remain with regulated providers;
- physical security/defense, public taxation, diplomacy and statutory administration are outside LOM platform authority;
- CI/documentation proves only defined/tested maturity, not live operational proof.

Canonical doctrine:
**Digital-First + AI-Native + Automation-First + Human-Centred + Evidence-Based + Secure + Auditable + Scalable**
