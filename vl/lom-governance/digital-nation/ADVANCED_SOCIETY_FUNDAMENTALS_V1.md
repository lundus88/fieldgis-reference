# LOM Virtual World — Advanced Society Fundamentals v1

Status: DRAFT / NON-PRODUCTION  
Date: 2026-09-29  
Owner: LOM Core Governance

## Purpose

Translate the twelve fundamentals of a durable, advanced society into the LOM Virtual World as **private-platform institutions, services and control systems**.

LOM Virtual World remains a digital society/platform. It does not claim sovereign statehood, legal citizenship, public taxation, legal tender, statutory courts, police/military powers, diplomatic recognition, public land title, or unlicensed financial authority.

The operating doctrine is:

**Digital-First + AI-Native + Automation-First + Human-Centred + Evidence-Based + Secure + Auditable + Scalable**

The member prosperity loop is:

**Member → Learn → Work → Earn → Hold lawful/platform assets → Build Business → Access regulated investment/capital channels where available → Create Value → Contribute → Prosper**

## Constitutional operating principle

A durable system must not depend on one person's constant intervention.

**No person or AI may bypass the active Charter, policy, authority, evidence and audit controls.**

Founder-led governance is preserved, but changes to rules must occur through an explicit, versioned, auditable amendment path. AI cannot self-amend authority, appoint itself as successor, or waive human gates.

## The 12 fundamentals

### 1. Rule of Law & Institutions → Charter, Rights, Contracts & Accountability

LOM analogue:
- Founding Charter;
- Policy & Rights Registry;
- member rights;
- clear platform rules;
- contract/offer/order evidence;
- dispute and appeal process;
- integrity/inspector function;
- immutable audit trail.

Operational formula:

**Rule → Authority → Evidence → Action → Audit → Appeal**

P0 requirement:
A consequential action fails closed if authority or evidence is unresolved.

### 2. Productive Economy → Market, Business, Work & Productivity

LOM analogue:
- LOM Market;
- Business-in-a-Box;
- Opportunity Exchange;
- Economic Participation;
- creator/digital IP economy;
- B2B commerce;
- Product Factory;
- productivity analytics.

Economic formula:

**Human Capability + Technology + Capital Access + Productivity → Sustainable Value Creation**

Primary outcome:
Members should be able to convert skills, products or services into accepted transactions and repeat economic activity.

### 3. Financial & Capital System → Regulated Rails + Treasury + Capital Adapters

P0:
- external regulated payment rails;
- transaction ledger;
- invoices/receipts/accounting evidence;
- earnings/commission records;
- platform treasury/budget controls;
- fraud and reconciliation controls.

P1/P2, only through compliant providers and separate review:
- financing/credit adapters;
- insurance adapters;
- savings-like or investment access through licensed providers;
- compliant escrow;
- international payouts.

Flow principle:

**Fast + Safe + Transparent + Auditable**

LOM does not become an unlicensed bank, lender, insurer, investment platform or currency issuer.

## Evidence maturity model

Each pillar must progress through evidence stages:

- **DEFINED** — documented contract/policy exists.
- **TESTED** — automated tests/guardrails pass.
- **PREVIEW_PROVEN** — controlled synthetic/Preview journey passes.
- **PILOT_PROVEN** — controlled real pilot produces auditable evidence.
- **PRODUCTION_PROVEN** — independently verifiable Production evidence exists.

CI PASS alone does not equal PILOT_PROVEN or PRODUCTION_PROVEN.
