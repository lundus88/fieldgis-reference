Privacy Notice

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 20 September 2026.

1. Who we are

LUNDUS DIGITAL SYSTEMS operates digital systems, automation, AI-assisted SaaS, geospatial and related technology services. Website: lundusdigital.com. Privacy enquiries may be sent to hello@lundusdigital.com. This Privacy Notice v1.0 is effective from 20 September 2026. LD has chosen not to use the SSM-recorded telephone or residential-overlapping trade address as its public-facing business contacts; a dedicated LD business phone and separate lawful public business/correspondence address remain launch prerequisites.

2. Personal data we may collect

Depending on the service and interaction, we may collect name, organisation, job or business details, email address, telephone number, location, service requirements, account identifiers, quotation/order references, transaction references, support communications, files or content submitted for a project, and technical information such as IP address, device/browser information, timestamps, security events and service usage.

3. Sources of personal data

We may obtain personal data directly from you through enquiry, quotation, onboarding, account, project, payment and support interactions; automatically through website or service technologies where enabled; from an organisation you represent; or from service providers and other lawful sources where relevant to the contracted service.

4. Purposes of processing

We may process personal data to respond to enquiries; assess requirements; prepare quotations and agreements; create and administer accounts; deliver contracted services; process and reconcile payments; provide customer support; communicate service or security information; prevent fraud and abuse; maintain audit, reliability and security records; improve our services using appropriately governed information; and meet applicable legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution or regulatory obligations.

5. Whether providing data is mandatory

Some information is required to prepare a quotation, enter into or perform a contract, authenticate an account, complete a transaction, deliver a service, investigate a support request, or meet legal obligations. If required information is not provided, we may be unable to proceed with the relevant request or service.

6. Disclosure to third parties

Where necessary and subject to appropriate controls, personal data may be disclosed to contracted hosting, cloud, authentication, communications, payment, security, analytics, support or professional service providers; auditors or advisers; a customer organisation you represent; regulators, courts or public authorities where required by law; or another party where you have directed or authorised the disclosure.

7. Payment information

Payment card or online-banking credentials are handled by the selected payment provider and are not intended to be stored by this website. LD may retain transaction identifiers, order references, amount/currency records, reconciliation status and related accounting evidence.

8. Cookies, analytics and technical data

Where enabled, cookies or similar technologies may support essential sessions, security, preferences, measurement and service reliability. Non-essential analytics or marketing technologies should be activated only under the applicable consent and configuration rules. This Release Candidate does not itself activate live Production data collection.

9. Cross-border processing

Some contracted technology providers may process data outside Malaysia. Where cross-border processing applies, LD will use applicable contractual, security and governance measures and follow Malaysian personal-data requirements.

10. Security and retention

We apply role-based and least-privilege access, authentication controls, logging, security monitoring, backup/recovery and other proportionate safeguards according to the service. Personal data is retained only for periods reasonably required for the stated purpose and applicable contractual, security, tax, accounting, dispute or legal requirements, then deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

11. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal data held about you, request correction of inaccurate or misleading data, and exercise available choices or restrictions concerning processing. Requests may require identity verification and may be subject to lawful exceptions.

12. Marketing

LD does not treat a service enquiry as blanket consent for unrelated marketing. Where promotional communications are used, they should follow the applicable consent, preference and opt-out rules.

13. Changes to this notice

Material changes will be versioned and dated. A later candidate does not silently replace an approved effective version; the public effective version must be explicitly approved under the LD Legal & Trust gate.
