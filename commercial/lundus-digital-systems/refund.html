Refund & Cancellation Policy

Candidate version: 1.0-RC2
Effective date: pending explicit human approval before public commercial launch.

1. Scope

This policy applies to LUNDUS DIGITAL SYSTEMS commercial digital services when activated, subject to the accepted quotation, order, Statement of Work (SOW), subscription or product-specific terms and any mandatory rights under applicable law.

2. Before work starts

If an order has been paid but governed project work has not started, a cancellation request may be reviewed against the accepted scope, payment status, provider fees and third-party costs already committed. Any refundable amount must be supported by the authoritative order and payment records.

3. After work starts

For custom services, completed work, approved milestones, committed capacity and non-recoverable third-party costs may affect the refundable amount. A material scope change is handled through the Change Request process rather than being silently treated as a cancellation or refund entitlement.

4. Subscription or digital access

Where a SaaS or subscription service is offered, product-specific cancellation, renewal and refund rules should be displayed before payment. Cancellation of future access, account closure and any monetary refund are separate controlled actions.

5. Third-party and non-recoverable costs

Domain, hosting, cloud, software licence, payment-provider, data, professional service or other external costs already incurred or committed may be non-recoverable where the provider does not refund them. Such treatment must be consistent with the accepted commercial agreement and applicable law.

6. Duplicate, incorrect or unauthorised charges

Suspected duplicate, incorrect or unauthorised charges should be reported promptly and investigated using the provider transaction reference, internal order record and payment reconciliation evidence. LD will not rely on a browser redirect as proof of a completed charge.

7. Refund method and processing

An approved refund should normally be returned through an appropriate traceable method consistent with the original payment route and provider capabilities. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or financial institution; the customer should receive a reference or acknowledgement for an approved refund action.

8. Chargebacks and disputes

Customers should contact LD first so the transaction can be investigated. Fraud, abuse, chargeback and dispute handling may require preservation of relevant order, payment, delivery, communication and security evidence.

9. How to request

Send cancellation, refund and billing-support requests to support@lundusdigital.com with the relevant quotation/order reference and sufficient information to identify the transaction. Do not send card credentials or online-banking passwords.

10. Mandatory rights

This policy does not exclude rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded. Where a mandatory rule conflicts with a contractual provision, the mandatory rule prevails to the extent required.

11. Versioning and effectiveness

Material changes will be versioned and dated. A candidate version is not effective merely because it exists in the repository or Preview. Public effectiveness requires explicit human approval under the LD Legal & Trust gate.
