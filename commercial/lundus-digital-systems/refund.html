Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: to be confirmed before public commercial launch.

Before work starts

Where an order has been paid but work has not started, a cancellation request may be reviewed against the agreed scope, third-party costs already incurred and applicable law.

After work starts

For custom services, completed work and committed third-party costs may affect the refundable amount. Project-specific terms in the accepted quotation or agreement also apply.

Digital or SaaS access

Product-specific cancellation and refund terms should be shown before payment. Access reversal, account closure and refund processing are treated as separate controlled actions.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

Verified duplicate or incorrect charges should be investigated using the provider transaction reference and internal order record.

How to request

Refund, cancellation and billing-support requests may be sent to support@lundusdigital.com. Mandatory rights under applicable law are not excluded by this policy. This Release Candidate is not yet an effective public policy version.
